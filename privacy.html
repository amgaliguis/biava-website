PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated on November 20, 2026

Information We Collect

BIAVA does not require customer accounts or online payment processing. When you place an order or send an inquiry, we collect the basic details you voluntarily share in your email, such as your name, email, address, phone number, delivery address, and shirt order details.

How We Use Your Information

We use the details you send to our email strictly to confirm your orders, coordinate delivery and payment instructions, and respond to your inquiries.

Data Protection & Sharing

Your privacy is important to us. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We only share necessary delivery details with our courier partner to ship your orders safely to your doorstep.

Your Rights & Contact

You have full control over your personal information. You can ask us to update or delete your order records from our email logs at any time by contacting us.
